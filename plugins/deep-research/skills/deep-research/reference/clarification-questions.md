# Clarification Questions Reference

Use AskUserQuestion to establish the user's goal, prior knowledge, focus, and region
before planning; the question counts below are the budget.

## Universal questions (always ask 2-4):

1. **Research goal** — "What is the purpose of this research?" (Buying decision /
   Learning / Professional need / Comparing options / Fact-checking / Life decision)
2. **Existing knowledge** — "What do you already know about this topic?" (free text)
3. **Specific focus** — "Are there specific aspects to focus on or exclude?" (free text)
4. **Regional context** — "What region/country context matters?" (if not already clear)

## Domain-specific questions (ask 2-3 based on detected domain):

**Health**: Researching for whom? Existing conditions? Prevention/diagnosis/treatment?
**Finance**: Experience level? Risk tolerance? Amount/time horizon? Personal or business? Country of residence/tax jurisdiction? (Critical for UCITS/instrument availability and tax treatment)
**Consumer**: Budget range? Primary use case? Brand preferences/deal-breakers? New only or open to refurbished?
**Home & Garden**: Location/climate zone? DIY or professional? Scope/budget? Existing or new?
**Travel**: Dates/season? Trip duration (how many days)? Travel party (solo/couple/family/group, ages of children if any)? Departure city? Budget per person? Travel style (backpacker/mid-range/luxury)? Main interests (culture, nature, food, beach, adventure)? Accommodation preference (hotel, Airbnb, hostel, resort)? Dietary restrictions or food preferences? Mobility concerns?
**Sports**: Training or spectating? Fitness level? Injuries/limitations? Equipment available?
**Cooking**: Dietary restrictions? Skill level? Equipment? Everyday or special occasion?
**Career**: Career stage? Industry/role? Country? Salary/job market/skills/workplace?
**Legal**: Jurisdiction? Personal or professional? Active matter or general research?
**Science/Education**: Background level? Current research or established knowledge? Course/project/general?
**Parenting/Pets**: Children, pets, or both? Ages? Health/behavioral concerns?
**Entertainment**: Events, reviews, recommendations, or general info? Location? Genre preferences?

## Quick mode exception

For **quick** mode: reduce to 1-2 essential questions only. For standard/deep/ultradeep:
ask the full set. If answers reveal additional ambiguity, ask **one follow-up round**
of 1-2 questions.
