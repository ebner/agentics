## Preferred Source Tiers

### Tier 1 — Always search and prefer
| Source Type | Examples | Use For |
|---|---|---|
| Pediatric associations | AAP, RCPCH, DGKJ, WHO child health | Guidelines, milestones, safety |
| Veterinary associations | AVMA, BVA, AVSAB | Pet health, behavior, nutrition |
| Government health agencies | CDC milestones, CPSC recalls | Developmental norms, safety data |
| Peer-reviewed journals | Child Development, JAVMA, J Vet Behavior | Evidence-based findings |
| Animal welfare (vet-backed) | ASPCA, RSPCA, PDSA | Pet care, poison lists, welfare |

### Tier 2 — Good secondary sources
| Source Type | Examples | Use For |
|---|---|---|
| AAP parent resources | HealthyChildren.org | Parent-facing guideline summaries |
| Established parenting guides | What to Expect, KidsHealth (Nemours) | Age-stage overviews |
| Vet-reviewed pet resources | PetMD, VCA guides, iCatCare | Pet care guides |
| Certified behaviorists | CAAB, ACVB, CCAB certified professionals | Behavior modification |
| Breed registries | AKC, The Kennel Club, breed-specific clubs | Breed info, health testing |

### Tier 3 — Use with caution
| Source Type | Examples | Caveat |
|---|---|---|
| Parenting communities | r/Parenting, BabyCenter forums | Anecdotal; verify with guidelines |
| Pet owner communities | r/dogs, r/cats, breed forums | Anecdotal; verify with vet sources |
| Parenting blogs | Named authors with some credentials | Check qualifications, look for citations |
| Pet food company resources | Brand-specific guides | Commercial bias; check AAFCO standards |
| Social media professionals | Instagram/YouTube trainers/parents | Verify credentials; entertainment bias |

## WebFetch Prompts by Source Type

**For pediatric association guideline pages:**
"What specific guidelines or recommendations does this page provide? Include the
target age range, the strength of the recommendation (if stated), the date of
publication or last update, and any specific thresholds or criteria. Note if this
is a clinical guideline versus parent-facing advice."

**For veterinary association pages:**
"What specific veterinary recommendations are provided? Include the species and
breed applicability, the evidence basis cited, any dosage or frequency guidance,
and conditions requiring professional veterinary consultation. Note the publication
or last review date."

**For developmental milestone pages:**
"What specific developmental milestones are described, for what age range, and how
are they categorized (motor, cognitive, language, social-emotional)? Note whether
milestones are presented as averages, ranges, or minimum thresholds. Include any
red flags or 'when to be concerned' criteria."

**For pet breed information pages:**
"What specific breed characteristics are described including temperament, exercise
needs, grooming requirements, common health issues, lifespan, and suitability for
families with children? Note the source authority and whether health claims cite
breed health surveys or veterinary data."

**For parenting approach/method pages:**
"What parenting approach or method is described, what age range does it target,
what is the evidence basis (citations to research), and what specific techniques
or strategies are recommended? Note the author's credentials and any stated
limitations or situations where the approach may not apply."

**For pet nutrition pages:**
"What specific nutritional recommendations are made, for which species and life stage?
Does the page reference AAFCO, FEDIAF, or NRC standards? Are specific nutrients,
portions, or feeding frequencies mentioned? Note any foods flagged as toxic or
harmful, and whether the advice comes from a veterinary nutritionist."

**For child safety / product recall pages:**
"What specific safety hazard or product recall is described? Include the product
names, model numbers, date ranges of affected products, the nature of the hazard,
reported injuries, and the recommended consumer action. Note the issuing authority
(CPSC, EU Rapex, etc.)."

**For animal behavior science pages:**
"What specific behavior is addressed, what is the recommended intervention or
training approach, and what is the evidence basis? Does the page distinguish between
evidence-based methods and anecdotal approaches? Note any certifications or
credentials of the author."

## Temporal Sensitivity Rules

| Topic | Sensitivity | Guidance |
|---|---|---|
| Vaccination schedules | Updated annually by advisory committees | Always cite current year's schedule |
| Developmental guidelines | Stable (updated every 5-10 years) | Note edition; CDC updated milestones in 2022 |
| Pet food recalls | Ongoing, can happen any time | Check FDA pet food recalls for current year |
| Child product safety recalls | Ongoing | Check CPSC (US), EU Rapex for current data |
| Car seat guidelines | Updated with new research (every few years) | Verify current AAP/NHTSA guidance |
| Breed health data | Evolving as genetic testing improves | Note survey year and sample size |
| Training methodology | Evolving but slowly (decades) | Note when debunked methods are still popular |
| Screen time guidelines | Updated periodically by AAP | Note the guideline year |
| Pet nutrition standards | AAFCO updates periodically | Note the edition year |
| Parenting research | Ongoing; landmark studies remain relevant | Note study date and replication status |
